Our Beliefs

Founded on the Word of God. Rooted in faith, truth, and restoration.

📄 Full Faith Statement

1. The Authority of Scripture

We believe the Holy Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments are the inspired Word of God and the final authority for faith, doctrine, and Christian living. (2 Timothy 3:16–17, Psalm 119:105, Hebrews 4:12)

2. The Nature of God

We believe in one eternal God revealed in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. These three are co-equal and co-eternal. (Deuteronomy 6:4, Matthew 28:19, 2 Corinthians 13:14)

3. Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, fully divine and fully human. He was born of the Virgin Mary, lived a sinless life, died for our sins, rose again on the third day, ascended to heaven, and will return again. (John 1:1–14, Isaiah 53, 1 Corinthians 15:3–4)

4. The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit convicts the world of sin, regenerates believers, dwells within them, and empowers them to live holy lives and serve God. He is the third person of the Trinity who indwells believers, empowers them for service, and guides them into all truth. (John 14:16–17, 26, Acts 1:8, Romans 8:26–27, Galatians 5:22–23)

5. The Filling of the Holy Spirit

We believe the Church and every believer can be filled with the Holy Spirit. The filling of the Holy Spirit empowers the Church for holy living, spiritual gifts, bold witness, and effective ministry. (Acts 1:8, Acts 2:4, Ephesians 5:18, 1 Corinthians 12:4–11)

6. Humanity and Sin

Human beings were created in the image of God but fell into sin. Because of sin, humanity is separated from God and needs redemption through Jesus Christ. (Genesis 1:27, Romans 3:23, Romans 6:23)

7. Salvation

Salvation is a gift of God received through faith in Jesus Christ. It includes justification, regeneration, sanctification, and eternal life. (Ephesians 2:8–9, Romans 10:9–10, Titus 3:5)

8. Water Baptism

We believe in water baptism by full immersion for believers who have confessed faith in Jesus Christ. Baptism symbolizes the believer's identification with the death, burial, and resurrection of Jesus Christ. (Matthew 28:19, Romans 6:3–4, Acts 2:38)

9. The Lord's Supper

We believe in the celebration of the Lord's Supper as a remembrance of Christ's sacrifice on the cross. Believers partake of the bread and the cup in remembrance of His body and blood until He returns. (Luke 22:19–20, 1 Corinthians 11:23–26)

10. The Church

We believe the Church is the body of Christ composed of believers who gather for worship, teaching, fellowship, and mission to the world. (Matthew 28:19–20, Acts 2:42, Ephesians 4:11–13)

11. Church Leadership – The Five Ministries

We believe Christ established leadership gifts in the church: apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors, and teachers to equip the saints and build the body of Christ. (Ephesians 4:11–13)

12. Marriage and Family

We believe marriage is ordained by God as a covenant between one man and one woman. The family is the foundational unit of society and should be nurtured according to biblical principles. (Genesis 2:24, Matthew 19:4–6, Ephesians 5:22–33)

13. The Second Coming of Christ

We believe Jesus Christ will return personally and visibly in glory. At His return, the dead will be raised and Christ will establish His kingdom. (Acts 1:11, 1 Thessalonians 4:16–17, Revelation 22:12)

14. Resurrection and Eternal Judgment

We believe there will be a resurrection of both the saved and the lost. Those who have trusted in Christ will receive eternal life, and those who reject Him will face eternal judgment. (John 5:28–29, Revelation 20:11–15)

15. The Five Pillars of Church Life

  • Apostles' Teaching (Acts 2:42)
  • Fellowship (Acts 2:42)
  • Breaking of Bread / Communion (Acts 2:42)
  • Prayer (Acts 2:42)
  • Apostolic and Prophetic Foundation with Christ as the Cornerstone (Ephesians 2:20)

Mission Declaration

Restoration Community Church exists to restore lives through the Gospel of Jesus Christ, build strong families and leaders, equip believers for ministry, and serve communities with compassion.
