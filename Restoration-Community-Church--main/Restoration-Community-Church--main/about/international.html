Our Global Ministry

Reaching hearts and communities across continents through visual stories

Restoration Community Church is committed to extending the Gospel beyond our local community. Through our international ministry partnerships, we serve and support church plants and communities in Tanzania and the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC).

Led by Pastor Banza Mukalay's vision and with the support of our congregation, we are dedicated to discipleship, community development, and spiritual restoration in these regions.
