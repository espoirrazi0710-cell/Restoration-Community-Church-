Mission Statement

Why we exist

Restoration Community Church exists to glorify God by making disciples of Jesus Christ through biblical teaching, loving fellowship, prayer, and the breaking of bread — empowering believers to live transformed lives and impact their communities with the Gospel.

We believe that every person is created in the image of God and deserves to experience His grace, healing, and purpose. We exist to welcome the broken, strengthen the faithful, and send the equipped — so that our community and the world may be transformed by the power of the Gospel.

Welcome to Our Family

I am so glad you are here — whether you are returning home to faith or taking your very first step, you have found a place where you truly belong.

Our name is not just a title — it is a calling. We believe that God is in the business of restoration. Restoring families. Restoring hope. Restoring dignity to those who have felt forgotten. Every Sunday, every prayer, every act of service we offer is a declaration that no life is too broken for God to redeem.

My prayer is that when you walk through our doors, you feel the warmth of a community that will walk alongside you through every season of life. We do not have all the answers, but we are committed to seeking them together — in love, in truth, and in faith.

Come as you are. You are welcomed, you are loved, and you are needed here.

— Pastor Banza Mukalay
